SUMMARY

“I will become Hokage one day... Believe it!” Naruto Uzumaki, an outcast of the Hidden Leaf Village , is determined to one day become the Hokage, the leader of that village. Because the Nine-Tailed Fox is sealed within him, Naruto's upbringing was one of isolation. But his days of loneliness end when he carrying out missions as a member of Team 7, comprising of Sasuke, Sakura, and the team's Jonin leader, Kakashi!
