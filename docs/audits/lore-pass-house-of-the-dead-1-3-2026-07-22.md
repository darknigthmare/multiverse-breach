# Lore pass - House of the Dead 1-3 - 2026-07-22

## Scope

The three standalone universes now use named series entities instead of generic zombie labels.

| Universe | Heroes | Regular threats | Bosses | World boss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| House of the Dead | Thomas Rogan, G, Sophie Richards | Sam Type A-1, Name Type G-2, Kenfis Type F-1 | Chariot, Hangedman | Magician Type-0 |
| House of the Dead 2 | James Taylor, Gary Stewart, Amy Crystal | David, Ebitan, Devilon | Judgment (Kuarl and Zeal), Tower | Emperor Type Alpha |
| House of the Dead 3 | Lisa Rogan, veteran G, Dan Taylor | Mark, Rogan Commando, Cain | Death, Fool | Wheel of Fate |

The generic `Emperor Prototype` name was corrected to `Emperor Type Alpha`. Generic equipment and Resident Evil-like herb or antidote terminology was replaced by AMS firearms, ammunition, rescue equipment, EFI access material and BioReactor research material.

Rogan Commandos now use their canonical dual combat knives and kicks instead of a generic firearm profile. Cain is described and animated as the EFI human-plant hybrid with vine-like arms, and Fool remains the dark-haired sloth-like Type 0028 rather than an ape or a literal jester.

## Visual and gameplay rules

- The first game stays anchored to Curien Mansion and the underground DBR laboratory.
- The second game uses the Venice-like Goldman incident, civilian rescue routes, canals and Goldman tower.
- The third game uses the ruined EFI facility, pump-action shotgun combat, the BioReactor and Wheel of Fate.
- Tarot bosses keep their own silhouettes and are not rendered as interchangeable zombies.
- Each game remains an independent DLC universe in the registry.
- Each universe exposes 17 available manifest entries: three heroes, three regular threats, two bosses plus one world boss, four item icons and four stage views.
