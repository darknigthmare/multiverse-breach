## Visual references

- [Official Dandadan anime character page](https://anime-dandadan.com/en/character/)
- [Official Japanese Dandadan character page](https://anime-dandadan.com/character/)
- [Official Golden Ball merchandise page](https://anime-dandadan.com/goods/1338/)

The official references establish Turbo Granny's white beckoning-cat vessel,
Seiko's practical baseball bat, Okarun's simple rectangular glasses, and the
small luminous golden spiritual collectible. The final icons are original
pixel-art interpretations and do not copy an official sprite.

## Generation and validation

- Generator: built-in OpenAI ImageGen, one call per item.
- Source treatment: uniform green chroma key, soft matte, despill, and a
  one-pixel edge contraction.
- Final format: `512x512` PNG RGBA.
- Alpha range: `0..255`.
- Transparent pixels have zeroed RGB values.
- No visible green chroma remains.
- One complete centered object per icon, with no character, duplicate, text,
  logo, frame, scenery, or cast shadow.
- All four final files were inspected at their project paths after alpha
  extraction and normalization.
