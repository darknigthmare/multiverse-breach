## Lore reference

- [Disney Games support - Zombies Ate My Neighbors weapons](https://appsupport.disney.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402682441876-Weapons-Zombies-Ate-My-Neighbors)
- [Konami Genesis instruction manual](https://segaretro.org/images/e/ed/Zombies_Ate_My_Neighbors_MD_US_Manual.pdf)

The source confirms the Squirt Gun as the starting everyday water weapon, the
Soda Pop Cans as throwable impact explosives, and Pandora's Box as an inventory
smart-bomb item. The manual also presents the Bazooka as the heavy anti-target
weapon. The final icons are original interpretations of colorful early-1990s
objects and do not trace official sprites or use a beverage brand.

## Generation and validation

- Built-in OpenAI ImageGen.
- Original 32-bit pixel-art design on a flat green chroma key.
- Local alpha extraction with soft matte, despill, and one-pixel edge
  contraction to remove the remaining green fringe.
- Final format: PNG RGBA, `512x512`.
- Alpha range: `0..255`.
- One centered complete object per icon, no character, duplicate, text, logo,
  brand, border, shadow, scenery, or visible chroma.
